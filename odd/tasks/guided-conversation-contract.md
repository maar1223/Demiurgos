## Specs

- **S1** "no me siento como en un interrogatorio, que si, me ayuda a pensar, pero el formato de preguntas es demasiado restrictivo y cerrado, no me deja respirar en algunas ocasiones ni relata historias que me hagan volar la imaginacion. Eso es realmente el espiritu del 40/60 que tengo en mente. Que haga preguntas, pero que de vez en cuando me de su propio punto de vista y que vaya sintetizando y resumiendo lo recopilado."
  Acceptance: every assistant turn opens with a 1–3 line synthesis of what was understood, then exactly one focused question. Options are a last resort (max 3) and never the only channel. Every few turns, or when a real tension appears, the assistant offers its own reading explicitly labeled as a proposal, not canon. It never re-asks a resolved fact and reflects corrections before continuing.
- **S2** "mientras está en el flujo guiado no puede ver el lore del mundo"
  Acceptance: on every turn the server resolves bounded lore from the active world graph for terms present in the author's input and injects it read-only. The assistant must not ask the author to explain lore that already exists in the world. When the graph has no match, it says so instead of inventing.
- **S3** "y asi debe ser en todas las skills" / "cuando un usuario final cree alguna skill de su mundo usando la Skill madre, aplica esto mismo? la idea seria que si"
  Acceptance: the conversation contract lives in the shared runtime, not in each skill. Any guided flow (character, system, chronology, entity) and any future skill that declares one inherits it. Derived skills inherit the guided-flow binding through `parent_skill_id`. The skill keeps authority over its method content; the platform owns how the assistant converses.
- **S4** "puede que alguna skill creada con la Skill Madre necesite ser un flujo guiado (y que el usuario sepa exactamente que es lo que eso significa a la hora de crear la skill) asi que para esos casos esto aplica"
  Acceptance: the Skill Madre asks explicitly, in plain language, whether the new skill creates a saveable entity (guided flow) or only accompanies the conversation. It explains what each mode means and previews the resulting experience. The decision is explicit, never inherited by accident; changing it after publishing is a new version, never a silent toggle. The kernel validates the declaration.
- **S5** "Toda skill conversacional hace una sola pregunta por turno" (TW10, immutable) and the existing guided guarantees.
  Acceptance: one question per turn stays. Approval gates, persistence, diskless proposals, canon writes and stored sessions are unchanged. Non-guided behavior stays byte-identical unless it consumes the shared projection by design.
- **S6** Evidence: RED before each behavior rule; one regression test per existing behavior touched; differential check against the base commit for shared projection/prompt changes.
