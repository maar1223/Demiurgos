## Naming Convention

All files in this directory use **lowercase snake_case** (`[0-9a-z_]+\.md`). This is the durable naming contract for the Demiurgos project — enforced for all templates, including future imports from community repositories.

Underscore-prefixed files (`_`) are templates to copy, not reference catalogs.
